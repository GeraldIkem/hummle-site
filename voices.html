Meet the readers.

Ten original voices to start, and many more on the way. Each one keeps who they are while the story changes how they speak: quieter when someone hides, faster when someone runs, unsteady when someone is about to cry.

  • Mara

    North American English

    Warm and unhurried. A middle-low voice with a little natural grain. Fear makes her quieter, never colder.

    “The kettle had gone cold an hour ago, and still she sat by the window…”

    Best for almost anything. One of the two default narrators.

    North American English

    Resonant and present. Rounded and articulate, never a movie-trailer voice. Urgency adds pace, not volume.

    Many more voices on the way.

    Ten is just the start. New readers join as each one passes our listening tests: more ages, more accents, more characters to cast. Other languages come later.

Every voice is original; none copies a real person. The portraits are AI-generated to show each voice’s character. Audio previews arrive as each voice is finished and approved.
