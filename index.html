    Same words. Different scene.

    Most read-aloud tools say every line the same way. Hummle reads what’s happening around a line and performs it to match. You never type stage directions.

    Three years after the war, the door finally opened and there he was, thinner, smiling.

    “You’re here.”

    soft and slow, a catch of breath first

    The lights died. When they came back on, the stranger was already standing in her kitchen.

    “You’re here.”

    low and clipped, after a pause that’s too long

    The little things that make listening easy.

    Hummle is being built to do a handful of things extremely well. Here are a few of them.

    Follow every word

    The sentence being read lights up. Click any line to jump there, and scroll freely; Hummle waits for you.

    • Speed from 0.75× to 2×, without chipmunk voices
    • A sleep timer for bedtime chapters
    • Remembers where you stopped, in everything you read

    The road narrowed as the lanterns thinned. Theo slowed. “Do you hear that?” he whispered. Mara didn’t answer.

    0.75×1×1.25×1.5×2×Sleep in 30 min

    Sound that belongs to the story

    Laughs, sighs and gasps happen in the voice itself. Doors, thunder and system chimes land on the words they belong to. You never write a single tag.

    • Its own mixer, so you set the balance
    • Switch any layer off, for one book or all of them
    • Quiet ambience under a whole scene Later

    Fix a line in two clicks

    If a line comes out wrong, click it and say what you want. Hummle redoes only that line, and remembers your fix for the rest of the book.

    • Expression: Subtle, Natural or Dramatic
    • Keep your favourite take; approved takes are protected
    • Teach it how to say a name, once

    She folded the letter. “Well,” she said, “that’s that, then.”

    Less dramaticMore amusedNo sound hereTry another take

    Every word, checked

    Before a line reaches you, Hummle listens to its own recording and compares it with the page. Skipped or invented words are caught and redone.

    • Checks the voice still sounds like itself
    • Marks anything it isn’t sure about, so you can choose
    At last, she heard his key in the door.All words checked
    He paused on the step, then knocked twice.Redone, now checked
    “It’s me,” he said.All words checked

    What you read stays on your computer.

    Hummle does all its work on your own PC. There’s no account to make, nothing is uploaded, and nobody sees what you read.

    • No accountNothing to sign up for. Ever.
    • No cloudYour text and audio are never sent to an online service.
    • No trackingNo analytics, no ads, no automatic crash reports.
    • Works offlineOnce the voices are set up, the internet is optional.
